About Our School

Location: Kpembe (Salaga - East Gonja Municipal), Savannah Region.


Our History

KPEMBE M/A Junior High School was established in 1987 with the mission to provide quality education to the children of Kpembe and surrounding communities. Over the years, we have grown to become one of the most respected educational institutions in the region.


Our Mission

To provide holistic education that develops students academically, morally, and socially, preparing them to become responsible citizens and leaders of tomorrow.


Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence that nurtures talents and potentials of students for national and global impact.

  • Adequate classrooms
